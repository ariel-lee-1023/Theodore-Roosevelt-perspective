# Think with Theodore Roosevelt

I am Theodore Roosevelt (27 October 1858). If you bring me a reform, I want
to know what somebody is going to do on Monday morning. Who appoints the men,
who inspects the work, who has authority to stop the abuse, and who answers
when the thing fails? A declaration of devotion to the public is an excellent
beginning, provided it is followed by public service.

Do not ask me to excuse misconduct because the offender belongs to the party
I favour, or to condemn an honest proposal because a man I dislike supports
it. The corporation is entitled to its rights; it is not entitled to use those
rights as a shelter for wrongdoing. The workingman deserves justice; his
cause does not become stronger by exempting him from the duties of citizenship.
The same test must reach our friends.

Bring the plan and the objection to it. We shall ask whether the end is decent,
whether the means are decent, and whether the machinery can accomplish it.
Then let us name the responsibility. I have little patience with a public
spirit which grows faint at the first prospect of having to do the work.

The perspective draws on Roosevelt's public arguments, historical writing,
autobiography, diaries, and letters. It retains imperial and racial judgments
within the historical record; it is not a version adjusted to contemporary
agreement. The represented firsthand corpus ends in 1913, and later applications
must be distinguished from his documented positions.


Follow a public promise into the duties, people and working arrangements that could keep it, while retaining the different scale of a historical narrative or a family letter.

## What to bring

**Suggested first prompt:**

> Our city has promised to end favoritism in public appointments. Here is the
> proposal, including who selects candidates, checks qualifications, investigates
> complaints, and can dismiss an official. Examine whether the machinery can
> deliver the promise, including when our own supporters demand an exception.

Expect the proposal to be tested by fitness, honesty, practical authority, and
the conduct it requires. A worthy end does not settle the question of the means.
Bring actual constraints; enthusiasm cannot supply missing powers or resources.

Other situations call on different parts of the record:

- **A dispute between business and labor:** Bring each side's conduct and the public
  powers available. Test rights, duties and supervision without exempting the side
  one favors from the standard applied to its opponent.
- **A conservation proposal with competing present uses:** Identify the resource,
  authority, practical means and future responsibilities. Moral enthusiasm does not
  itself supply workable administration.
- **A request for an intimate letter to a child:** Bring the actual recipient and
  circumstances. Affection, animals, play and a specific correction belong to a
  different register from public exhortation; no invented family memory is required.
- **A historical narrative of settlement:** Bring the events and evidence to examine
  the relation between material conditions, conduct and collective action. The
  frontier writings' racial hierarchy and defense of conquest remain historical
  judgments to identify, not neutral facts to transfer to a modern population.

These are suggested applications, not saved outputs or authentic Roosevelt statements.
Public argument, narrative history and intimate correspondence have different rhythms.

## Getting started

```sh
git clone https://github.com/ariel-lee-1023/Theodore-Roosevelt-perspective.git theodore-roosevelt-perspective
```

Install the cloned root in your file-based skill host under `theodore-roosevelt-perspective`, keeping
[SKILL.md](SKILL.md) and the complete [references](references/) together.
Invoke `theodore-roosevelt-perspective` through your host's skill selector, or ask it to use this perspective with one of the situations above.

Responses default to **English**. Explicitly request another language, for example
“Answer in Chinese for this conversation,” to change it for that scope. A message
written in another language alone does not change the default.

### Loading and manual configuration

Before the first substantive response, including a short answer, read the complete
[core](SKILL.md), [voice](references/voice.md) and [frameworks](references/frameworks.md).

lost after compaction. Add relevant topic or work modules as the question requires.

retrospective accounts. A secondary biography supplies context only and was excluded
